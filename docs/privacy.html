Mental Health Tracker

Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

Mental Health Tracker ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our iOS application.

Information We Collect

Mental Health Tracker is designed with privacy in mind. We collect and store the following information:

  • Mood Entries: Your daily mood selections, text notes, and voice recordings are stored locally on your device and synced to your personal iCloud account.
  • Dates: The dates associated with your mood entries.

All data is stored using Apple's Core Data framework and synced via iCloud, which means your data is encrypted and stored in your personal iCloud account, not on our servers.

How We Use Your Information

Your data is used solely to provide the core functionality of the app:

  • To display your mood history in the calendar view
  • To sync your data across your devices using iCloud
  • To allow you to review and reflect on your mood patterns

We do not collect, sell, or share your personal data with third parties. We do not use analytics or tracking services that would identify you personally.

Data Storage and Security

All your data is stored locally on your device and synced to your personal iCloud account. This means:

  • Your data is encrypted using Apple's iCloud security measures
  • We do not have access to your personal data
  • You can delete your data at any time by deleting the app or clearing your iCloud data

Voice Recordings

Voice recordings are stored locally on your device in the app's Documents directory. If you choose to sync via iCloud, recordings are included in your iCloud backup. You can delete voice recordings at any time through the app interface.

Third-Party Services

Mental Health Tracker uses the following Apple services:

  • iCloud: For syncing your data across your devices. This is optional and can be disabled in your device settings.
  • Core Data: For local data storage on your device.

We do not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data at any time through the app
  • Delete your data by deleting the app or clearing your iCloud data
  • Disable iCloud sync in your device settings

Children's Privacy

Mental Health Tracker is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the App Store or via email if provided.
